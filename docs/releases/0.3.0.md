### Play and presentation

- A disc selector opens when the game is started without `--disc`, lists recent and nearby discs and real optical drives, and the game can now read a disc straight from a drive.
- Launching straight into a level starts at that level's own start point with the loadout the campaign would have given by then.
- The end of each unit shows the original uplink sequence, the levels raise their own mission orders, and the title plays its own attract demos.
- Creatures fire from their guns: traced rounds, machinegun bursts, dying guards firing where they face, flying bolts and rockets, and muzzle flashes and reports for every round.
- Rockets, grenades, the hand grenade and the BFG ball are their own models, with trails, fireballs and explosions; the railgun leaves its spiral, and pickups sparkle from the item itself.
- Controller rumble follows the console's own patterns for weapons, hits, explosions and the Tank Commander's footsteps (off by default, as on the console).
- SCREEN POSITION moves the picture, the television shows 240 lines, and an unplugged controller brings up the console's own screen.

### Gameplay

- The three attract demos on each disc, European PAL and North American NTSC, now replay the console's own pad recordings tick for tick to their ends. The random-number stream and the player's position match the console's own code on every frame.
- Getting there corrected the frame order (creatures, items, doors, projectiles and the AI clock), creature hearing, sight, aim and pursuit, knockback, melee, rails, grenades, rockets, megahealth decay, door collision and many smaller details. `CHANGELOG.md` lists them all.
- RESTART LEVEL returns to where the level began, and RESUPPLY AND RESTART resupplies.

### Tools and validation

- `tests/fixtures/lockstep_oracle.py` runs the console's own game loop over an attract demo, on either disc (`--ntsc` for the US one), and records every `rand()` call with its caller.
- New `q2psx-inspect` reports (`loadout`, `zonescript` button operands, model scale) and more `--report` counters; `--pause`, `--restart`, `--shoot-to-kill` and `--shot-display` help capture specific moments headlessly.
- The changes in this release were checked against the unit tests, the disc-backed client suites on both discs, and the Windows (MSVC) and Linux (GCC and Clang) builds. The release archives are built and tested on Windows x64, Linux x64 and macOS Apple silicon, with SHA-256 checksums.

### Known limitations

This remains a work in progress, and the 1.0 boundary in `docs/RELEASING.md` is still awaiting sign-off. Measurements that need retail hardware, such as GPU timing, final audio levels, movie A/V phase and physical controllers, have not been made. Deliberate differences from the console are listed in the player guide.
